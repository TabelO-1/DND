**The Veldraxian Adventurer's Guild**: The Adventurer's Guild holds modest of notable influence throughout the Kingdom, maintaining a guildhall in all the major cities. Through these halls, adventurer's are guided toward work that serves the needs of the realm, and its people. Whether escort jobs, exploration, or dealing with threats beyond the scope of the ordinary folk.

Renown System in use: DM's Guide page 22.

## Prestige Ranks:

(S+): Royal Platinum Rank, 10000 Renown.
(S): Platinum Rank, 1000 Renown
(A): Gold Rank, 250 Renown
(B): Silver Rank, 100 Renown 
(C): Iron Rank, 50 Renown
(D): Copper Rank, 10 Renown
(E): Provisional Rank, 0 Renown

Guild Missions or other recognized commissions increase Prestige Ranks

Stonebridge Branch:
Led by Guildmaster Marrek

Currency trades:
The Guild operates as a bank in a limited capacity, one of those functions is currency trades. They charge 1 piece per 100 transfered, ie if you turned 100 silver into 10 gp, they charge 1 silver. If you turned 100 gp into 10 plp, they'll charge a gold. They do not have the capcity to turn currency into Royal Platinum Coins.
