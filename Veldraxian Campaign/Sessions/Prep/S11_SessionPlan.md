# Session Eleven
***Enough.***

**DM Excerpt**

Session 11 should close the Brill Gang Arc, without needing to further burden the party on hunting down the rest of the gang. Their attack on the tavern, killed and captured members, and the recovered ledger have given the Guard enough information on the gang to begin dismantling it. The organization that has stood for a decade will soon be gone.

The session's goal is to: reward the party for their efforts in Stonebridge (Promotion), return the story to the original line (bringing attention back to the disturbances), reveal the wider mystery of Aeris' murder, and level the party up to 3.

## Opening Scene

The Following morning within the Central East Guard Tower. The party has rested in the Tavern overnight and Eldid is reviewing the ledger. The captured gang members are under interrogation within the cells below. Eldid will inform them"The blow you dealt yesterday will likely end this gang. Arrests are already ocurring with the information inside and revealed by the prisoners. He will explain that the Gang isn't gone, and the Gang's core is in hiding, but the damage is being dealt. Some members have fled, cut ties, others are turning on one another, and Businesses that once paid protection money have begun speaking to the Guard. The party is thanked for their tenacity in this case, with this openning, the authorities can now move in.

During the conversation, another guard enters the office, interupting,

"Sir! There's someone here. Says he needs to speak to the Sparrow Fledgings."

Eldid will ask the party if they're expecting someone, and if not, ask if they wanted to go out, or to send him in.

**The Messenger**

The messenger walks in/is outside, a young male human not in the best of clothing. He appears eager to leave.

The Man keeps his hands visible at all times, saying quietly, "I was paid...*pause*...to deliver this."

He hands them a folded letter. If outside he departs, if inside he will attempt to go, if kept he doesn't have much to say, he was paid, he was paid not to ask, he was told where to go and when.

"
Enough.

Meet me tonight. 

Be someplace alone if you want answers.

- Marlo

"

**Preparation for the Meeting**
The party will decide how they intend to attend.

They may send one person, the party may come alone, they may follow from a distance, involve the Guard, Eldid, or turn the meeting into a needlessly elaborate operation.

Marlo expects deception, but he still desires to speak with them before the Brill Gang drags him down with the rest.

**The Meeting**

Marlo is not eager to fight. He knows the Gang is losing, the Tavern fell, arrests are being made, and the Guard now possesses enough to at least criple the Gang. Whether protecting himself, attempting to stop total collapse, or simply refusing to allow himself to go down with Brill, his reason now doesn't matter.

He's a humanoid in a dark cloak, sounding male, you can see long hair underneath the cloak, he tries not to ever face you.

"In three days, you've done more damage than the Guard has managed in ten years."

He confirms that Aeris' murder was arranged by him, but not because Aeris' happened to anger the Gang somehow, and not because Brill wanted him dead.

"I was paid to have him killed."

There was described a Bronze Dragonborn that was staying at the Yellow Wyvern Tavern that night. He assigned the work, made payment for the killers, and recorded its completion, but his death did not originate from him. He never met the employer, only met a messenger. The only thing that is memorable is the payment.

He produces a coin, a **Royal Platinum Coin**.

Marlo explains: Such coins are technicially legal currency, but their value makes them extraodrinarily uncommon in ordinary trade. They are typically associated with large transactions, aristocratic wealth, royal institutions, old fortunes, and people rich enough that gold is inconvient. Each one bears the King's Seal, and the Kingdom's promise to always accept their value.
