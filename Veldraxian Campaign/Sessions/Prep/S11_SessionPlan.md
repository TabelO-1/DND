# Session Eleven
***Enough.***

**DM Excerpt**

Session 11 should close the Brill Gang Arc, without needing to further burden the party on hunting down the rest of the gang. Their attack on the tavern, killed and captured members, and the recovered ledger have given the Guard enough information on the gang to begin dismantling it. The organization that has stood for a decade will soon be gone.

The session's goal is to: reward the party for their efforts in Stonebridge (Promotion), return the story to the original line (bringing attention back to the disturbances), reveal the wider mystery of Aeris' murder, and level the party up to 3.

## Opening Scene

The Following morning within the Central East Guard Tower. The party has rested in the Tavern overnight and Eldid is reviewing the ledger. The captured gang members are under interrogation within the cells below. 

“The blow you dealt yesterday will probably end the Brill Gang. Arrests are already underway using the ledger and what the prisoners have told us. Their leadership has gone into hiding, members are fleeing or turning on one another, and businesses that paid them for protection are finally speaking to us.”

“The gang isn’t gone yet, but it won’t recover from this. You gave us the opening we needed.”

During the conversation, another guard enters the office, interupting,

"Sir! There's someone here. Says he needs to speak to the Sparrow Fledgings."

Eldid will ask the party if they're expecting someone, and if not, ask if they wanted to go out, or to send him in.

**The Messenger**

The messenger walks in/is outside, a young male human not in the best of clothing. He appears eager to leave.

The Man keeps his hands visible at all times, saying quietly, "I was paid...*pause*...to deliver this."

He hands them a folded letter. If outside he departs, if inside he will attempt to go, if kept he doesn't have much to say, he was paid, he was paid not to ask, he was told where to go and when.

"
Enough.

Tonight, be alone if you want answers.

I'll find you.

- Marlo

"

**Preparation for the Meeting**
The party will decide how they intend to attend.

They may send one person, the party may come alone, they may follow from a distance, involve the Guard, Eldid, or turn the meeting into a needlessly elaborate operation.

Marlo expects deception, but he still desires to speak with them before the Brill Gang drags him down with the rest.

**The Meeting**

Marlo is not eager to fight. He knows the Gang is losing, the Tavern fell, arrests are being made, and the Guard now possesses enough to at least criple the Gang. Whether protecting himself, attempting to stop total collapse, or simply refusing to allow himself to go down with Brill, his reason now doesn't matter.

He's a humanoid in a dark cloak, sounding male, you can see long hair underneath the cloak, he tries not to ever face you.

"In three days, you've done more damage than the Guard has managed in ten years."

He confirms that Aeris' murder was arranged by him, but not because Aeris' happened to anger the Gang somehow, and not because Brill wanted him dead.

"I was paid to have him killed."

There was described a Bronze Dragonborn that was staying at the Yellow Wyvern Tavern that night. He assigned the work, made payment for the killers, and recorded its completion, but his death did not originate from him. He never met the employer, only met a messenger. The only thing that is memorable is the payment.

He produces a coin, a **Royal Platinum Coin**.

"Royal Platinum. Legal coin, but you won't find one changing hands in a market. Nobles use them. Royal offices use them. Old fortunes use them."

He places the coin in your hand.

"Whoever wanted your friend dead had money. Real money."

"Whatever you do now, take this as your reason to leave me out of it."

**End of Marlo's Role**
Depending on the party's choices, Marlo's fate can be decided right here.

If the plan was to arrest him then and there, then whenever the signal is given and he's surrounded, he will surrender himself. In custody he will offer the necessary information to take down the rest of the Gang for Immunity. If they refuse the deal, one night he'll simply disapear from his cell.

If the party let him go, he'll leave Stonebridge, and his fate is left unknown.

## Stonebridge's Reaction

After the meeting, the following morning, the consequences of what the party has accomplished will come to be.

Business reopen in the Eastern District where shutters once remained closed. Guards conduct arrests, Steven's Place has many Guards at it. Former victims come forward, and more members flee. People begin to recognize the party. People point, watch the party as they go, and mutter their name "Sparrow Fledglings." Rumors claim the Party defeated fifty Gangsters, blew up a tavern, or singlehandedly captured Brill. None of it is true, but it is still amazing publicity for the party. The story goes: No matter how it happened, the Sparrow Fledglings in days of arriving in Stonebridge ended what the town had endured for a decade.

**Guild Recognition**

In the following morning, before they left the tavern, a letter would await for them. It's from Marrek, asking them to come to the Guild. After arriving, heads will turn towards the party entering and people whisper and watch. Marrek comes out, saying, "The Guild Recognizies good work, regardless of whether it was issued here." And he hands you all a newly marked guild card.

"You protected Stonebridge, exposed a criminal organization, and I've got half the Guard Captains asking for your aid in arrests. Congrats, you've been promoted." As the party picks up their cards, it marks you a Copper-Rank Adventuring Party. A small appluse is given by the Guildhall for the party, and the Guildmaster asks you follow him to his office.

**More than just a Promotion**

"What you did here matters, you've helped our city. You don't look like the beginners who first entered this hall, you carry yourselves differently now, more certain, more capable."

OOC: You all reach Level 3. We'll discuss choices here in a moment.

"But it isn't why you've come here, why the Grandfather sent you to me."

He reaches into one of the many filing cabinets, pulling out a few papers, setting them on the desk for you all to read.

"I worked with the Grandfather before taking this post, I know something of what he's looking for."

Reading the Papers, you see three reports.

**Silence in the Elderward**: Hunters north of Stonebridge report that larger wildlife has abandoned an entire stretch of the forest. Birds and animals alike have vanished. No carcasses, tracks of a predator, or obvious cause have been found. The forest is simply empty.

**Hollowmere's Bear Problem**: Bears previously reported attacking livestock have moved dangerously close to the village, and several villagers have now been mauled. The animals normally remain within the Elderward, but something appears to be driving them outward.

**The Crossroads**: Farmers surrounding the village report that their livestock refuse to leave their enclosures. Even when the gates are opened, the animals panic and retreat indoors.

"Individually, I'd call them peculiar. Together, they sound familiar."

Allow the players to ask questions, debate, and choose a path, or none. But the Session Ends here in the office.
