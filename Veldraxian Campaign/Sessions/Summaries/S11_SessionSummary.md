# Session Eleven
***Mystery in the Tavern: Who ate Shadow's Hot Chocolate?***

The following morning, the Sparrow Fledglings gathered inside the Central Eastern Guard Tower, where Eldid was reviewing the information uncovered from the raid at Wolf Tavern. Between the ledger and the four surviving gang members currently being interrogated in the cells below, the Stonebridge Guard finally possessed enough information to begin dismantling the Brill Gang.

The organization would not disappear overnight. Its core leadership had gone into hiding, many of its members remained free, and much of its influence was still embedded throughout the city. Even so, Eldid was confident that the damage could not be undone.

Arrests were already underway. Some members had fled Stonebridge entirely, others were beginning to turn on one another, and businesses that had paid protection money for years were finally willing to speak with the Guard.

The conversation was interrupted then by a Guard entering the room, informing them of a messenger that had arrived for the Sparrow Fledglings. The party was not expecting visitors, but invited him in.

The young man clearly did not want to be there, but informed them he had been paid to deliver a letter to them, and quickly turned to leave after giving Shadow vague answers. Shadow intercepted him hallway, blocking his path, demanding more thorough answers, but the unfortunate truth was the only thing the young man knew was that he had been given the letter, instructions, and payment.

Novadia had also followed into the hallway and attempted to calm the situation by showing the messenger a magic trick using her cards. Shadow eventually allowed the man to leave, returning to the office and accidentally leaving Nova alone in the hallway.

Tolin soon came to her rescue and agreed to participate.

Nova placed four cards on the floor, asked Tolin which row contained her chosen card, then asked which column it was in. She picked up the card occupying both positions, proudly declared that she had performed a magic trick, and asked whether the card was Tolin’s.

**It was.**

Back in the office, Shadow opened the letter and then read it aloud.

*"Enough. Tonight, be alone if you want answers. I'll find you. - Marlo"*

---

The Party and Eldid relocated to the Yellow Wyvern Tavern to discuss the implications of the message. Nova ordered her usual apple cider. Quenlith ordered a meal, which consisted of a potato, cooked zucchini, and sushi. Shadow requested a random beverage and was given hot chocolate topped with whipped cream and sprinkles.

At some point, Shadow left the table to visit the Restroom. When he returned, his hot chocolate was gone.

Not merely empty, no. The cup itself had disappeared.

Quenlith, who had whipped cream on her face, immediately denied responsibility before Shadow could even accuse her. Nova also insisted she had done nothing, despite her earlier comments about eating cups and the suspicious ceramic fragments scattered in front of her.

No confession was ever obtained. The Mystery of who ate Shadow's hot chocolate remains unsolved.

Returning to the matter of Marlo, Shadow and Eldid concluded that the Guard could not be present, they could not even remain nearby. If Marlo detected official involvement, he might refuse to appear.

The Sparrow Fledglings would have to handle the meeting alone. With that, Eldid departed, taking his dino chicken nuggets and tomato soup with him, completely stealing the plate and bowl.

With the rest of the day available before nightfall, the party set out to complete several errands. Shadow visisted the Merchant's Quarter and sold several weapons seized during the road. He also purchased bolts for the crossbow he recovered there. Nova sold two daggers she had taken as well.

Tolin contributed by singing sea shanties, particularly while Shadow attempted to conduct business. Quenlith was there too. Shadow later searched for a magic shop, but the establishments he found appeared to be closed that day.

Along the way, they passed an elderly resident known locally as Crazy Joe, who loudly informed anyone willing to listen that the end times were approaching. No one paid him much attention.

---

That evening, the party chose a quiet courtyard in the Merchant’s Quarter, surrounded by buildings and reached only through narrow alleys. Shadow hid beneath a dark cloak on one side while Quenlith concealed herself opposite him. Nova sat openly in the center playing solitaire, and Tolin arrived later before circling the surrounding block.

As Shadow watched the alleys, he suddenly realized someone was standing less than five feet away. He had neither seen nor heard the figure approach and had no idea how long he had been waiting there.

Only after Shadow noticed him, did Marlo speak. "In three days, you've managed more damage than the Guard has done in ten years." Marlo was cloaked, sounded male, and kept his face turned away, though Shadow could see long hair beneath the hood. He admitted that he had arranged Aeris' murder. He received the contract, selected the killers, paid them, and recorded the job as complete, but he was not the mastermind they sought.

He was paid to kill him. The instructions came through a messenger, they identified a bronze dragonborn staying at the tavern that night. The only memorable part was the payment.

From beneath his cloak, he produced a Royal Platinum Coin and handed it to Shadow. Though legal currency, such coins were almost never used in ordinary trade. They were associated with aristocratic wealth, royal institutions, and ancient fortunes.

He told Shadow, "Someone with money wanted your friend dead. Real money. Whatever you do now, take this as your reason to leave me out of it." Shadow allowed him to go. Shadow and Quenlith briefly examined the coin, before the party returned to the Yellow Wyvern Tavern.

Shadow and Quenlith immediately went upstairs to sleep. Unfortunately, sleep proved difficult, as downstairs Nova and Tolin had begun another illegal solitaire gambling ring.

With Nova playing cards and Tolin enthusiastically taking bets, the pair managed to earn a respectable amount of money before the night ended. Once again, the Sparrow Fledglings had absolutely nothing to do with the illegal solitaire gambling ring occurring in their immediate vicinity.

---

Early the following morning, Shadow attempted to sprint directly to the Stonebridge Guildhall, leaving the rest of the party barely awake enough to prepare themselves, and dash after him. Tolin pursued while throwing rocks and obscenities in Shadow's direction.

As they raced through the streets, however, the party noticed something unusual. People were watching them, and not solely because a tabaxi was sprinting with surpising speed while an elf cashed him with stones. The citizens knew who they were. The party heard their named whispered as they passed, "The Sparrow Fledglings."

Rumors concerning their involvement in the Brill Gang's collapse had spread throughout the city. Some accounts remained reasonably close to the truth. Others claimed the party had defeated fifty gang members, destroyed an entire tavern, or personally captured Brill himself.

When they arrived in the Guildhall, conversations quieted. Adventurers and guild staff turned to watch them enter. Marrek emerged to greet them, "The Guild recognizies good work, regardless of whether it was issued here."

He presented the party with newly marked guild cards. "You've protected Stonebridge, exposed a criminal organization, and I've got half the Guard captains asking for your aid in making arrests. Congrats, you've been promoted."

The Sparrow Fledglings was now recognizied as a **Copper Rank Adventuring Party.**

Tolin immediately began singing a sea shanty, Novadia joining her with her innovative "Rush E", and Quenlith resuming her apparently well-established tap dancing career. A modest round of applause spread through the hall, as the party celebrated its promotion. The Guildmaster then asked them to enter his office.

---

Once the door closed, the Guildmaster turned serious, "What you did here matters. You helped this city."

"What stands before me now is an adventuring party, not the wannabes who first walked through these doors."

The Party has become stronger. "But that is not why you are here. Why the Grandfather sent you to me." He informed the party that he had once worked with the Grandfather, and knew something of the danger being investigated, although the Grandfather never told him everything.

Marrek opened one of the many filing cabinets in his room, producing a folder, and three reports he set upon the table for the party to read. He allowed them to examine them himself. Marrek said that together the reports sounded familiar

Between the three choices, Shadow was for the Crossroads, Nova for the Elderward, while Quenlith liked Hollowmere. With Tolin not giving an opinion, Shadow decided the path would be: Crossroads, Hollowmere, Elderward. Regardless of whether Crossroads was chosen first, Elderward should be last, as there weren't definitive things to search there, and would be more extensive of a quest.
