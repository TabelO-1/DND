# Session Twelve

***Carn(e Asada) and the Mystery of Jelbin***

The session began once again in Marrek’s office, where the newly promoted Sparrow Fledglings sat across from the Guildmaster. Marrek wasted little time reminding them why they were there.

“Better get on with it, Copper Ranks.”

Naturally, this led to Shadow and Novadia engaging Marrek in an unnecessarily long handshake before the party finally departed.

Outside, the party was briefly distracted by the fact that Quenlith now had a flying snake following her around. Nobody seemed entirely sure when this had happened or why. With this newest addition accepted with remarkably little resistance, the Sparrow Fledglings returned to the Yellow Wyvern to pack for their trip. Nova, maintaining tradition, stole all of the pillows once again.

From there they returned to the stables, retrieved their long cart and donkeys, and departed Stonebridge. They crossed the great stone bridge and continued south as the towering Standing Stone Mesas gradually came into view across the horizon.

By late evening, the party reached Jelbin Crossroads. The settlement was quiet, with most businesses closed and only the tavern still active. After getting their animals settled into the stable, the party proceeded to make enough noise outside that the Tavernkeep eventually stuck his head through the doorway and asked whether they intended to come inside or argue all night. They chose inside.

A room was secured and dinner followed downstairs, where Tavernkeep Carn—quickly renamed **Carn(e Asada)** by Tolin—served street tacos.

During dinner, Nova ate her wooden plate. Shadow told her not to eat the dishes. Nova defended herself by pointing out that she had eaten entire cups before. This immediately reminded Shadow of the still-unsolved disappearance of his hot chocolate and its cup back in Stonebridge. He accused Nova. Nova denied everything.

The mystery remained unsolved.

That night, the Sparrow Fledglings demonstrated their unique interpretation of sleeping arrangements. Nova constructed a pillow fort on the floor. Shadow climbed into the rafters. Tolin claimed the table. Quenlith entered her trance on one of the beds while sharpening her arrows, and her new flying snake received the other bed.

Tolin and Nova then proceeded to remain awake until nearly six in the morning, loudly talking, arguing, and entertaining themselves at the expense of everyone except Quenlith, who fortunately did not require silence to trance.

The following morning, neither Shadow nor the tavern appeared particularly pleased with them. Breakfast burritos were served while Shadow spoke with Carn about why the Sparrow Fledglings had come to Jelbin. Carn explained that the Village Elder would arrive around noon, leaving the party some time to wait. Shadow chose to spend part of this waiting period perched on top of a building outside before eventually returning when Elder Farin arrived.

Elder Farin sat down with the party and called over Farmer Ryl and Carn to help explain the problem.

Livestock throughout Jelbin had increasingly begun refusing to leave their enclosures for pasture. Farms farther west were suffering the worst of it, including Ryl’s farm, where the animals had almost entirely stopped going outside. Travel animals had also become increasingly difficult to manage, and farmers were beginning to worry about both lost income and shortages if their livestock stopped eating properly.

Nobody had found an ordinary explanation. The animals appeared healthy. They drank normally. There were no clear signs of disease or poisoning. Yet many had begun refusing food simply because they appeared too frightened to leave the places where they felt safe.

Carn added that travelers arriving from the west had occasionally complained about anxious or difficult horses, although experienced riders had generally managed to continue through without serious trouble.

With little else to work from, Shadow decided they should inspect one of the affected farms. Farmer Ryl led them to his own property. There, the problem immediately became obvious. Livestock crowded along the eastern sides of their pens, pressing themselves close to fences and structures as though trying to put as much distance as possible between themselves and the western countryside.

Tolin attempted to communicate with the cows. The cows were not particularly useful. They were scared, something was wrong, and they did not know what. Tolin slowly began losing patience and sanity attempting to get more information from them.

While the others remained at the farm, Shadow moved westward to scout the surrounding area. He eventually found tracks belonging to some large creature. Quenlith examined them and determined that they appeared to belong to some kind of bear.

Back at the farm, another clue surfaced. Part of Ryl’s fence had been chewed or damaged. Shadow decided the party should eventually camp on a hill to the west where he had discovered the tracks, but first they investigated the other farms around Jelbin.

The results were largely the same: Healthy animals, no obvious disease, and increasing fear. And the closer the farms were to the west, the worse the problem seemed to become.

That evening, Ryl invited the party to dinner with his family. The Sparrow Fledglings sat around the table with Ryl, his wife, Little Jimmy, Bob, Bob the Second, Jill, Tullert, and one additional child whose name apparently never became important enough to survive the evening.

Little Jimmy asked Quenlith why her ears were pointy. Nova attempted to impress the children with magic tricks. Tullert, the eldest, was unimpressed. Chimichangas were served.

After dinner, the party returned to the hill and made camp. They arranged watches between Quenlith, Nova, Shadow, and Quenlith again, as Tolin had immediately gone to sleep.

Quenlith noticed nothing unusual during the first watch. Nova later heard noises somewhere beyond the camp. When Shadow took his turn, however, he detected something large moving within roughly three hundred feet of the fire. He woke Tolin to accompany him. They were freighteningly close to sounding like they were getting along.

The pair moved quietly away from camp, Shadow occasionally becoming so stealthy that even Tolin lost track of where he had gone. Eventually they spotted it. Some kind of large creature stood out in the darkness, watching the campfire.

Shadow told Tolin to return to camp and wake Quenlith for her next watch in an hour, and then he followed the creature alone. He tracked it farther west as it moved through the darkness until it eventually stopped and released a distressed, screeching roar.

Rather than approach directly, Shadow began circling around it. The creature remained in place. Shadow continued moving until he reached a position roughly parallel with it, attempting to work his way completely around.

Then he stepped forward and something changed. A sudden wave of mental distress washed over him, dread, and the overwhelming certainty that something nearby was terribly, fundamentally wrong. Shadow managed to force the feeling down and remain in control.

The creature ahead of him still refused to move. With no clear understanding of what he had just encountered, Shadow abandoned the pursuit and began the roughly two-mile walk back toward camp. Whatever was frightening Jelbin’s animals was not simply a bear. And somewhere farther west, something was wrong.

## Quick Summary

The Sparrow Fledglings departed Stonebridge for Jelbin Crossroads after Marrek reminded the newly promoted Copper-Rank party to get moving. After an evening involving Carn(e Asada), street tacos, Nova eating another piece of tableware, an accusation regarding Shadow’s missing hot chocolate cup, and an almost entirely sleepless night, they began investigating Jelbin’s frightened livestock.

The animals were healthy but increasingly terrified of leaving their enclosures, particularly on western farms. Tolin’s attempts to question the cows established only that they were frightened and sensed something wrong. Shadow discovered large bear-like tracks and damage to Farmer Ryl’s fence, and investigation of other farms confirmed the fear worsened farther west.

After dinner with Ryl’s extremely populated household, the party camped on a western hill. During the night, Shadow discovered a large creature watching the camp and followed it farther west. While circling the distressed creature, he crossed into an area that suddenly filled him with an overwhelming supernatural sense of dread.

Shadow resisted the effect and returned toward camp, leaving the Sparrow Fledglings with their first direct evidence that something genuinely unnatural lies west of Jelbin Crossroads.
