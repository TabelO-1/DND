# Session 10
***We Didn't Start the ~~fire~~ Illegal Solitaire Gambling Ring!***

Following the chaos of the previous session, the party returned to immediately cause more chaos.

For a brief moment, silence settled over the tavern. Then the group pushed deeper into the building, converging on a closed door near the rear. Shadow attempted to force it open.

After a couple of attempts by Shadow to break the door down, inside awaited two Brill Gang members: a human seated behind a desk and a towering half-orc standing nearby. Both had already drawn their weapons.

The party rushed in.

Shadow moved first, striking quickly, while Nova charged the half-orc and attempted to bash him with her shield. This time, however, her target held firm and stopped her in her tracks.

The cramped room turned the fight into a bottleneck. The party struggled to squeeze through the doorway, while Eldid and the accompanying guards were again trapped behind the seemingly very murderous adventurers.

Quenlith fired arrows through whatever openings she could find. Shadow unleashed a flurry of blows against the half-orc, while Novadia repeatedly drove her shield into his face. The Orcish Man would fall to never awaken again.

The apparent leader of the safehouse then stepped forward. Armed with a longsword, he managed to land a solid hit on Shadow, one of the few attacks to successfully connect with the elusive tabaxi.

Then Tolin entered the room. She locked eyes with the leader and unleashed a particularly vicious insult:

“Your mother prefers a donkey over you, because at least the donkey has a successful career and the air hates you.”

The emotional (and psychic) damage was immediate. Then, for reasons understood only by Tolin, she began to cry.

Before the leader could recover from either the insult or the confusion, Nova rushed forward and smashed him unconscious with her shield. A brief silence followed.

Shadow moved upstairs to investigate, leaving behind the leader’s untouched cup of hot chocolate sitting on the desk. The party took a moment to catch its breath, during which Tolin and Novadia once again discussed the illegal solitaire gambling ring from the previous day, an event with which the party, naturally, had absolutely no involvement, they assured the Guards accompanying them.

The fighting wasn't over yet, as Shadow had begun to search the upper floor when three dwarven women stepped from one of the rooms. All three were armed, alert, and entirely ready to continue the battle.

Quenlith moved upstairs and prepared to fire into the fight while Shadow traded blows with the dwarves. Swinging his quarterstaff, he struck one of them hard enough to send her crashing through a window. She did not survive the fall.

Nova watched from the bottom of the stairs, briefly lost in thought, wondering for what reason had Tolin placed her father's head on the mantle years past, as she had claimed, before charging upstairs to join the fight.

Nova pulled Quenlith out of the way and attempted to do the same to Shadow. Shadow, however, held his ground. She then rushed forward and smashed one of the dwarves in the head, knocking her unconscious.

Or, as Novadia had previously established:

She had been “unconscioused.”

Shadow finished the fight by striking the final dwarf and knocking her out as well.

---

With the building finally secured and several gang members captured, Eldid at last managed to involve the Stonebridge Guard.

Twenty-four additional guards arrived to secure the scene and, for reasons that remained unclear, operate the bar.

While the guards processed the building, the party handled their own priorities.

Shadow quietly looted the upper floor and found a copy of the Stonebridge Ledger, which included records of the spontaneous illegal gambling operation from the previous day.

Novadia gathered the prisoners, Quenlith recovered whatever arrows she could, and Tolin continued crying.

The final count was substantial. Eleven Brill Gang members had been killed during what had originally been a reconnaissance mission. Four more were captured, including the safehouse leader.

On the leader, the party found a ring marked with a capital B and three stars. While Shadow and Eldid spoke, another guard approached with news. A concealed latch had been discovered in the storage room.

Shadow immediately rushed to investigate. Novadia followed shortly after and accidentally crushed a crate of apple cider, the very thing she had been thinking about since first seeing the tavern bar, leaving her slightly distraught.

Beneath the storage room, the party discovered no further enemies. Instead, they found the hidden center of the gang’s operations. The basement contained stolen goods, contraband, and, most importantly, a ledger.

Within it were several entries of immediate concern.

One confirmed that the “bronze dragonborn” had been handled and that payment had been issued for the completion of the job.

Other entries referenced the gang’s wider criminal operations, a warehouse, and an individual known only as Marlo.

With that finished, the remaining prisoners were transferred to the nearest Guard Tower, and everyone would have a much needed rest as the Guard handled things at the Tavern.

## Quick Summary

The party finished clearing the Wolf Tavern, defeating the remaining Brill Gang members and capturing several others, including the safehouse leader. Eleven gang members were killed, four were captured, and the Stonebridge Guard arrived in force to secure the building and, inexplicably, operate the bar.

A hidden basement revealed stolen goods, contraband, and a ledger confirming that Aeris had been deliberately targeted and that payment had been made for his murder. The ledger also pointed toward a warehouse and someone named Marlo, giving the party their next lead in the Brill Gang investigation.
